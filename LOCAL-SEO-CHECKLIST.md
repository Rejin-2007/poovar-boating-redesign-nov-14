## Profile setup and verification

- [ ] Claim or verify the correct Google Business Profile; avoid creating a duplicate listing.
- [ ] Use the real business name used at the location. Do not append “Poovar Boating” or other search terms unless they are part of the actual name.
- [ ] Choose the closest accurate primary category offered by Google for the real boating service. Add secondary categories only if they describe actual services.
- [ ] Set the actual customer-facing address or the eligible service-area configuration. Confirm that the map pin points to the real boarding place.
- [ ] Verify both phone numbers, website URL, email, and booking/message destination.
- [ ] Add operating hours only after the operator confirms the current schedule; update seasonal changes promptly.
- [ ] Complete Google’s verification process and keep profile ownership with the business.

## Photos, updates and reviews

- [ ] Upload current, genuine photos of the boats, boarding point, route and team. Use only images the business has permission to publish.
- [ ] Add an occasional update for service changes or seasonal information; avoid claims about availability unless current.
- [ ] Ask real customers for an honest review after a completed trip. Do not offer rewards, ask only happy customers, or write reviews for customers.
- [ ] Reply professionally to both positive and critical reviews without exposing customer details.
- [ ] Keep any linked social profiles official and active; do not add guessed profile URLs to the site or schema.

## Citations and local partnerships

- [ ] Keep business name, address, phone and website consistent across legitimate tourism listings and business directories.
- [ ] Correct old listings instead of creating additional entries.
- [ ] Pursue relevant, genuine tourism partnerships or local editorial mentions; do not buy bulk directory links.
- [ ] Review profile insights and Search Console periodically to see which queries and pages bring qualified enquiries.
